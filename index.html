About

I build software that ships — from database schema to deployed infrastructure, and increasingly, the AI layer in between.

I'm a Computer Engineering student and full-stack/backend engineer who has spent the last two years moving from coursework into production: real client software, hackathon-built platforms, AI-powered document tooling, and infrastructure I've deployed and paid for myself. That range — backend APIs, cloud infrastructure, applied AI, and the frontends that sit on top — isn't scattered; it's the same instinct applied at every layer: understand the system, then make it reliable.

Most recently that's meant designing REST APIs and services in Spring Boot and FastAPI, standing up PostgreSQL/MySQL-backed systems, containerizing them with Docker, and deploying to AWS with an eye on cost as much as uptime. Earlier work — a GST-compliant billing system for a real granite manufacturing client, a hostel booking platform running on a 12-service AWS deployment — taught me that production software has to survive contact with actual users and actual constraints, not just a demo.

I'm currently a Trainee AI Full Stack Developer at Glimora International, where I split time between backend services, React/Next.js interfaces, and integrating OCR and LLM workflows into applications people use daily.
